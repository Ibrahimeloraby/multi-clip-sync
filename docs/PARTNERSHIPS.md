## Partnership Email Templates

### General Affiliate Pitch
```
Subject: Affiliate Partnership — LoyaltyOne UAE Loyalty Aggregator

Dear [Name],

LoyaltyOne helps UAE consumers identify the best loyalty program to use at every
merchant. We're building the definitive guide to UAE loyalty earn rates.

[Program Name] appears frequently in our top recommendations. We believe an
affiliate arrangement would drive incremental card applications / subscriptions
to your program while giving our users confidence in the data they see.

We propose:
- You provide us an official earn-rate data feed (or regular updates)
- We display a verified badge on your program data
- We include a referral link for new applicants
- You pay a CPA of [AED X] per qualified referral

Our current metrics: [X] monthly active users, [X]% are UAE residents aged 25-45,
[X]% hold or have previously held a [program] card.

Happy to share a pitch deck. Would a 20-minute call this week work?

Best,
[Name] | Founder, LoyaltyOne
loyaltyone.ae | hello@loyaltyone.ae
```

### Data API Request
```
Subject: Official Earn-Rate Data Request — LoyaltyOne

Dear [Name],

I'm reaching out about LoyaltyOne, a UAE loyalty rewards aggregator. We currently
display [Program] earn rates at [X] UAE merchants based on community-contributed data.

We would like to ensure our data is accurate and up-to-date. Specifically, we're
looking for:

1. An official merchant partner earn-rate list (even a CSV/spreadsheet would be helpful)
2. A point-of-contact for future data updates when earn rates change
3. Permission to display the [Program] logo alongside earn rates in our app

In return, we are happy to display a "verified by [Program]" badge and drive referral
traffic. This is a no-cost arrangement unless you'd like to explore a deeper partnership.

Can you connect us with the right team?

Best,
[Name] | LoyaltyOne
```
